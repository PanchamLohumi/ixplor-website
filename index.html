Questions

Good to know

Is iXplor free?

Yes. iXplor is free to download and use.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones today: download the beta under Get the app. An iPhone version is on the way. Leave your email there and we'll tell you when it's ready.

Does it only work in India?

iXplor is built in India and tuned for Indian cities first, but place briefings, nearby search and Drops work anywhere you travel.

How is my Travel DNA made?

From the places you save as Drops and the traveller tags you pick. AI turns that into your archetype, your vibe and a suggested next stop. The landscape mix is counted directly from your Drops.

Who can see my profile?

Other explorers in CoXplor see your name, photo, bio, tags, Travel DNA, your Drops (shown to about 1 km) and roughly how far away you are. Never your email or exact location.

Is the AI always right?

No. AI can get details wrong, so double-check opening hours and facts before you go. If something looks wrong or offensive, tap Report under it and we'll review it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: MyXplor → Edit Passport → Delete account. Or follow the steps on the delete account page.

Installing the beta

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download iXplor-Beta.apk above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
