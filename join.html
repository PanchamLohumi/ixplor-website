Get iXplor on your Android phone

Two quick taps and you're in. Open this page on your Android phone, signed in to the Google account you use for the Play Store.

  1. 1

    Join the iXplor explorers

    Tap the button, then tap Join group on the page that opens.

    Join the explorers
  2. 2

    Install from Google Play

    Come back here and tap the button. Accept on Google's page, then tap Download it on Google Play and install.

    Get iXplor on Google Play
  3. 3

    Open iXplor and start exploring

    Sign in with Google, drop your first pin and meet your Travel DNA.

Can't see iXplor on Google Play?

Make sure you finished step 1 with the same Google account your Play Store uses, then open step 2 again. It can take a few minutes to show up.
